Reflection Questions

1. Which textures did you choose and why?

I chose stone and cobblestone textures for the ground and castle because they fit the medieval courtyard theme. I used wooden textures for the gate, crates, barrel, and banner pole. I also used a red leather texture for the banner because it added some color to the scene.

2. How did normal maps improve your scene?

The normal maps made the ground and stone surfaces look like they had more depth and detail. They helped the surfaces look less flat without adding more geometry.

3. Which object benefited the most from texture mapping?

I think the castle walls benefited the most from texture mapping because the stone texture made them look much more realistic than the original plain gray material.

4. What effect did roughness maps have on lighting?

The roughness maps changed how light reflected from the surfaces. They made the ground, wood, and castle stone look less perfectly smooth and gave them a more natural and worn appearance.

5. Did texture tiling improve or hurt realism?

Texture tiling improved the realism of the scene. It prevented the ground texture from being stretched across the entire courtyard and made the pattern appear at a more realistic size.

6. If you had more time, what additional textures would you add?

If I had more time, I would add metal textures to parts of the gate and barrel, more detailed textures for the banner pole, and possibly moss or dirt textures on parts of the castle to make it look older and more detailed.
